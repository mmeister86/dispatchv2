## Export, erasure and restore obligations

TASK-33 must include all variants, optional legacy/current publication pins, current pointer and spent IDs, not only visible successful candidates. Traverse exact source IDs in dependency order; distinguish protected payload removal from financial/publishing facts and bounded consumed-identity markers. Do not retain sensitive payload merely because its row was described as immutable. Bounded immediate masks/physical local cleanup are implemented and independently tested; whole account/workspace export/deletion, user-identity erasure, external provider copies/backups and audit retention are not certified by those tests.

TASK-36 must restore references consistently and reproof current workspace/account/actors, original deadlines, purpose generation, metric/analyzer policy and settled receipts. TASK-44 adds original check→proposal→evaluation→derived-profile references and current evaluator authority; restoring any of them does not renew source deadlines, rerun a completed public request or activate a profile. Restored models/control/consent/cache are not renewed approval. Preserve consumed monitoring identities while their original candidate remains; do not reopen a trial by restoring one row without its marker. Restore never recreates an absent publication pin, resubmits a confirmed post, extends source retention or repairs historical semantics using current profiles.
