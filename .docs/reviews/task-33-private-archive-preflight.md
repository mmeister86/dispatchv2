Do not turn these into successful skipped rows.

SKIP protected payload only with directly established loss: deleted/deleting conversation; explicitly removed source or sourceUnavailable; finite original/current expiry; known cleanup pending/complete; absent already-cleaned component selected through a positively scrubbed domain root. No hidden row count/reason/skeleton. Directly requested missing root should reject. Standalone authorized operational order/financial headers may survive loss but must be a separately documented DTO, not a claim of available original content. A 30-day operational-retention setting does not renew or replace these source/actor proofs.
